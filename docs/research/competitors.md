# Research: how real Malaysian e-invitations look (2026-11-04)

Looked at live pages in the browser. Weddie's sample (`shairaahxshahideen.weddie.my`) is a real couple's invitation behind a 4-digit PIN, so it was not opened. Weddie's own marketing pages were not found by search.

## EkadInvite (ekadinvite.my), a direct competitor
- **Positioning:** "kad jemputan digital yang beritahu siapa akan datang". Weddings, aqiqah, birthdays, corporate, Hari Raya, **Tahlil & Doa Arwah**, many more types.
- **Offer:** 325+ designs, ready in under 10 minutes, one-time price: **Gold RM29, Diamond RM69** (no subscription), 48-hour refund.
- **How they sell:** gallery of designs, filter by style, **tap a design and the live sample card opens in a modal ("Pratonton langsung") with "Buka penuh"**. A phone mock-up on the hero shows a real card, not a UI.
- **The card itself (sample "Engraved Rose Deco"):**
  - Full-screen cover that looks like paper doors: engraved floral corners, gold line ornaments, wax-seal monogram (A & F), wide-spaced capital serif ("THE WEDDING OF", names, date).
  - A **"Specially invited: <guest name>"** tag (personal link), then an "Open invitation" pill. Doors part, confetti hearts fall over the floral frame, then the content.
- **Inside the card (from their feature list):** names and parents, event details (several events in one link, e.g. Akad + Resepsi), live countdown, Google Maps + Waze + copy address + parking/surau notes, one-tap RSVP with adult/child counters, digital guestbook, photo gallery, background music or YouTube, add to calendar, contact numbers, closing doa, BM/EN/bilingual. Diamond adds DuitNow gift QR, VIP personal note, "our story" timeline, programme, livestream links.

## What this means for us
1. A demo must **feel like an invitation card** (cover, seal/monogram, ornament, paper, wide-spaced serif type), not like a web app screen. My first demo page was the wrong thing.
2. The **live sample opens from a tappable preview** (modal with "open full"); the demo gallery is the main sales tool.
3. Competitors' price is public (RM29 / RM69). We show "contact us for a quote", which is the CEO's decision; the CEO should know the benchmark.
4. Our difference stays the **Google Sheet the host owns** and general events; theirs is a dashboard and 325 designs.
